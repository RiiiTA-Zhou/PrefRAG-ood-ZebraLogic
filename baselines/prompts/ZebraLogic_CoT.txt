Example #1
## Context:
There are 2 houses, numbered 1 to 2 from left to right, as seen from across the street. Each house is occupied by a different person. Each house has a unique attribute for each of the following characteristics:
 - Each person has a unique name: `Eric`, `Arnold`
 - Each person has a unique type of pet: `cat`, `dog`
 - The people are of nationalities: `dane`, `brit`
 - Each person has an occupation: `doctor`, `engineer`
 - People have unique favorite sports: `soccer`, `basketball`
 - Everyone has a unique favorite cigar: `prince`, `pall mall`

## Clues:
1. The person who has a cat and the Prince smoker are next to each other.
2. Eric is directly left of the person who owns a dog.
3. The Dane is not in the second house.
4. Arnold is the person who is a doctor.
5. The person who loves basketball is not in the second house.

## Headers:
["House", "Name", "Pet", "Nationality", "Occupation", "FavoriteSport", "Cigar"]

## Reasoning:
Clue 2: Eric is directly left of the dog owner → Eric = House 1, dog = House 2 (Arnold has dog).
Clue 3: Dane not in House 2 → Dane = House 1. So Eric is Dane, Arnold is Brit.
Clue 4: Arnold is doctor → House 2 = doctor. So House 1 = engineer.
Clue 5: Basketball not in House 2 → Basketball = House 1. So House 2 = soccer.
Remaining pet for House 1: cat (dog is House 2).
Clue 1: Cat owner (House 1) and Prince smoker are adjacent → since only 2 houses they are always adjacent, but they must be different people. So House 2 = prince, House 1 = pall mall.

## Output:
```json
[
  {"House": "1", "Name": "Eric", "Pet": "cat", "Nationality": "dane", "Occupation": "engineer", "FavoriteSport": "basketball", "Cigar": "pall mall"},
  {"House": "2", "Name": "Arnold", "Pet": "dog", "Nationality": "brit", "Occupation": "doctor", "FavoriteSport": "soccer", "Cigar": "prince"}
]
```

Example #2
## Context:
There are 5 houses, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right, as seen from across the street. Each house is occupied by a different person. Each house has a unique attribute for each of the following characteristics:
 - Each person has a unique name: `Alice`, `Bob`, `Arnold`, `Peter`, `Eric`
 - People have unique favorite sports: `tennis`, `baseball`, `soccer`, `basketball`, `swimming`

## Clues:
1. The person who loves tennis is not in the fifth house.
2. The person who loves swimming and Alice are next to each other.
3. The person who loves swimming is directly left of the person who loves tennis.
4. Eric is the person who loves soccer.
5. Eric is in the first house.
6. Arnold is in the second house.
7. Peter is the person who loves baseball.
8. Alice is not in the third house.

## Headers:
["House", "Name", "FavoriteSport"]

## Reasoning:
Clue 5: Eric = House 1. Clue 4: Eric loves soccer → House 1 = soccer.
Clue 6: Arnold = House 2.
Clue 3: Swimming is directly left of Tennis → possible pairs: (1,2), (2,3), (3,4), (4,5).
  But House 1 = soccer, so (1,2) is impossible. Tennis cannot be in House 5 (clue 1), so (4,5) is impossible. Remaining: (2,3) or (3,4).
Clue 2: Swimming and Alice are adjacent.
  If swimming = House 2, then Alice = House 1 or 3. House 1 = Eric, so Alice = 3. But Alice cannot be House 3 (clue 8). So (2,3) is impossible.
  Therefore swimming = House 3, tennis = House 4. Alice must be adjacent to House 3 → Alice = House 2 or 4. House 2 = Arnold, so Alice = House 4 (tennis).
Clue 7: Peter loves baseball. Remaining houses: 3 and 5. House 3 = swimming, so Peter = House 5 (baseball).
Remaining person: Bob = House 3 (swimming). Remaining sport for Arnold (House 2): basketball.

## Output:
```json
[
  {"House": "1", "Name": "Eric", "FavoriteSport": "soccer"},
  {"House": "2", "Name": "Arnold", "FavoriteSport": "basketball"},
  {"House": "3", "Name": "Bob", "FavoriteSport": "swimming"},
  {"House": "4", "Name": "Alice", "FavoriteSport": "tennis"},
  {"House": "5", "Name": "Peter", "FavoriteSport": "baseball"}
]
```

Example #3
## Context:
There are 4 houses, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right, as seen from across the street. Each house is occupied by a different person. Each house has a unique attribute for each of the following characteristics:
 - Each person has a unique name: `Eric`, `Peter`, `Alice`, `Arnold`
 - People own unique car models: `tesla model 3`, `honda civic`, `toyota camry`, `ford f150`
 - Each person has a unique birthday month: `jan`, `april`, `sept`, `feb`
 - Each person has a unique hobby: `painting`, `cooking`, `gardening`, `photography`

## Clues:
1. The person whose birthday is in January is not in the second house.
2. The photography enthusiast is somewhere to the left of Eric.
3. The photography enthusiast is somewhere to the left of Peter.
4. The person who owns a Honda Civic is directly left of the person who owns a Tesla Model 3.
5. There is one house between the person who owns a Tesla Model 3 and the person who enjoys gardening.
6. The person who owns a Tesla Model 3 is Arnold.
7. The person whose birthday is in February is the person who loves cooking.
8. The person who owns a Toyota Camry is Peter.
9. The person whose birthday is in April is Arnold.
10. Alice is the photography enthusiast.
11. Peter is the person whose birthday is in January.

## Headers:
["House", "Name", "CarModel", "Birthday", "Hobby"]

## Reasoning:
Clue 6: Arnold = Tesla Model 3. Clue 9: Arnold = April.
Clue 8: Peter = Toyota Camry. Clue 11: Peter = January.
Clue 10: Alice = photography.
Clue 1: January (Peter) not in House 2.
Clue 4: Honda Civic directly left of Tesla (Arnold). So Arnold cannot be House 1.
Clue 5: One house between Tesla (Arnold) and gardening → |pos(Arnold) - pos(gardening)| = 2.

Try Arnold = House 2 → Honda = House 1, gardening = House 4 (|2-4|=2).
  Peter (January, Toyota) cannot be House 2 (already Arnold) or House 1 (cramped). Try Peter = House 4 (gardening). Then Eric = House 3 (remaining person), Alice = House 1.
  Alice (House 1) is photography. Check clue 2: Alice left of Eric (H1 < H3 ✓). Check clue 3: Alice left of Peter (H1 < H4 ✓).
  Clue 7: February = cooking. Remaining birthdays: H1(Alice)=sept, H3(Eric)=feb, H4(Peter)=jan, H2(Arnold)=april. So Eric = feb.
  Hobby: H4(Peter)=gardening (from clue 5). H3(Eric)=cooking (feb=cooking). H2(Arnold)=painting (remaining). H1(Alice)=photography (clue 10). ✓
  Car: H1(Alice)=honda (clue 4). H2(Arnold)=tesla. H4(Peter)=toyota. H3(Eric)=ford f150 (remaining). ✓
All clues satisfied.

  {"House": "2", "Name": "Arnold", "CarModel": "tesla model 3", "Birthday": "april", "Hobby": "painting"},
  {"House": "3", "Name": "Eric", "CarModel": "ford f150", "Birthday": "feb", "Hobby": "cooking"},
  {"House": "4", "Name": "Peter", "CarModel": "toyota camry", "Birthday": "jan", "Hobby": "gardening"}
